Questions owners ask us

What happens if Sophie can't help a caller?

She takes their name and number and tells them your team will call back. Medical, billing and complaint questions always go to you.

Does it work with my calendar?

Yes. Every booking, change and cancellation is written to your Google Calendar automatically. Time off is added in SimplyBooked, so it is never offered to customers.

Do my customers need an app?

No. Everything happens by phone call, the button on your website, or text message.

Can a customer change someone else's booking?

No. Changes are only accepted from the phone number the booking was made with, or after the caller confirms the name or reference on the booking.

Can I try it before customers see it?

Yes. You start in test mode, with texts going only to your own numbers, and switch to live when you're ready.
